# Transport Management Role Assignment Matrix

| Role | Assign To | Main Responsibility | Typical Access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transport Operator | Dispatch desk, trip coordinators, branch operations users | Create and update transportation orders, assign vehicles and drivers, manage trips, fuel requests, and payment requests | Write access on operational transport documents, read access on masters and reports |
| Transport Manager | Transport head, operations lead, branch transport supervisor | Control routes, cargo setup, approvals, daily planning, and transport performance review | Full access on transport masters, operations, settings, and reports |
| Fleet Manager | Fleet compliance, workshop lead, vehicle readiness owner | Maintain fleet readiness, driver and trailer oversight, inspections, and compliance follow-up | Full fleet and transport oversight, including vehicle and driver masters |
| System Manager | ERP administrator, implementation partner | Migration, fixtures, troubleshooting, user setup, and platform-level administration | Full system administration across the stack |

Recommended assignment:

- Give `Transport Operator` to day-to-day desk users.
- Give `Transport Manager` to the business owner of dispatch and routing.
- Give `Fleet Manager` to users responsible for vehicle readiness, inspections, and compliance.
- Keep `System Manager` limited to administrators.
